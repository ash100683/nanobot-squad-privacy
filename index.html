Nanobot Squad privacy policy

Last updated: Nov 26, 2026

Nanobot Squad does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your quiz progress, stars, points, the squad bots you have met and your settings are saved on your phone only. Nanobot Squad does not send them anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It uses no analytics and no tracking.
  • It makes no network requests. All questions are inside the app.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

Nanobot Squad is made for students in grades 6 to 12. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing to share or delete on a server.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and tap Reset progress, or delete the app. Both remove everything the app saved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
